Magic Potion Mixer

13 jan 20252025 年 1 月 13 日

这篇文章写于在学校的课程期间，原文是英文。

Overview

The “Magic Potion Mixer” project combines multiple sensors and output devices to create an interactive and interesting experience. Users can pour liquid into the mixing platform, and the sensors will detect the colour, temperature, and liquid level of the liquid, triggering various effects such as changes in lighting, sounds, or even simulating “steam” effects.

Project Components

• Colour sensor: Detects the colour of the liquid.

• Temperature sensor: Measures the temperature of the liquid.

• Liquid level sensor: Detects the amount of liquid in the container.

• RGB LED light strip: Displays colour changes and effects according to the properties of the liquid.

• Buzzer: Emits sounds according to certain conditions.

• Arduino Uno/Nano: Serves as the central controller.

• Transparent container: Serves as the mixing container for the potion mixing platform.

Interactivity

User Input

The “Magic Potion Mixer” provides a rich interactive experience through real-time sensor feedback and effect output. Users participate in the mixing by choosing liquids of different colours. The colour sensor captures the colour of the liquid in real time and triggers effects through the output devices. Users can freely choose liquids of different colours, and this free input allows participants to experience the joy of creation during the mixing process. Users adding hot or cold water will affect the temperature of the liquid, which is related to the response of the LED light strip. Liquids of different temperatures produce different effects. For example, cold liquids will trigger cool-coloured lights, while hot liquids will trigger warm-coloured lights. Through the response of the temperature sensor, the liquid level sensor ensures that the system can correctly perceive the change in the height of the liquid when the user pours the liquid in. Different liquid levels trigger different lighting effects, which can determine whether the potion is successful or not, making each operation of the user generate different feedback.

System Feedback

Lighting is an important feedback mechanism of this system. Through the change in the colour of the liquid detected by the colour sensor, the LED light strip will immediately change its colour, providing intuitive visual feedback. For example, red liquid may trigger red lights, and yellow liquid may trigger yellow lights. Such changes in lighting not only allow users to see the reaction but also enhance the interactivity of the system, making it more immersive. Then, the buzzer emits different sound effects according to the properties (colour, temperature, and liquid level) of the liquid. This sound feedback combined with the lighting creates a multi-sensory interactive experience. When the liquid meets a specific “formula”, the sound effect emitted by the buzzer will become richer or change, simulating the reaction process of the potion.

User-friendliness

It is easy to operate. Users can participate in the mixing through simple operations (such as pouring liquid, adjusting temperature). The system provides instant feedback based on data such as the colour, temperature, and liquid level of the liquid. Users can enjoy a complete interactive experience without complicated operations. Intuitive feedback. The colour and sound feedback mechanisms allow users to immediately know whether their operations conform to the correct “magic formula”. This instant feedback helps build users’ trust and enables them to have expectations for the system’s responses.

Summary

The “Magic Potion Mixer” is a project that integrates sensor technology, creative interactive design, and user experience. I have focused on intuitive feedback, immersion, and personalised choices to provide users with a rich multi-sensory experience. Through rich visual, auditory, and physical feedback, users can enjoy the fun in creation and exploration and experience the wonderful effects brought by interactive technology.
