A.I. Artificial Intelligence

这篇文章写于在学校的课程期间，原文是英文。

Overview

A.I. Artificial Intelligence tells the story of David, a young robot boy with emotions. After being adopted by a couple, he hopes to become a “real child” to win the love of his adoptive mother. The film explores the complex relationship between humans and artificial intelligence, as well as how technology, ethics, and emotions are intertwined.

David is designed as a robot with intense emotional needs, which makes him obsessed with “love”. His emotions make humans feel ambivalent about his identity: Is he a “machine” or a “human”? I realise that when designing artificial intelligence, endowing it with emotions can have a double-edged effect. On the one hand, emotions can make AI closer to humans and provide a more human-like interaction experience. On the other hand, emotions can trigger ethical dilemmas. For example, should humans take the responsibility to meet the needs of machines? And should we treat robots as real humans equally?

The robot technology in the movie is created by humans, yet many human characters fail to offer genuine care to the robots. After being abandoned, David experiences great loneliness and pain. This makes me wonder whether, as the creators of technology, humans have the obligation to be responsible for the “state of existence” of artificial intelligence. Should we establish clear ethical frameworks for their perceptions and behaviours?

The movie presents a future dominated by technology, with issues such as human environmental damage, the improper use of technology, and social inequality running throughout. This makes me realise that technological progress must go hand in hand with social responsibility and strike a balance. Otherwise, it may bring irreversible negative impacts. In future technological projects, we need to pay more attention to the long-term effects of technology on the environment and humanity.

While learning about user experience and narratology in the course, this movie has offered me a new perspective. Artificial intelligence requires not only technological breakthroughs but also interaction models that align with ethical and social values. Through its powerful narrative approach, the film enables the audience to empathize with artificial intelligence. This inspires me to let users more deeply understand the essence of technology through storytelling when designing games or interactive projects.

My next learning goal is to explore how to effectively incorporate emotional and humanistic elements into AI projects, making technology closer to users’ needs and creating a more profound interactive experience. Meanwhile, I hope to delve into the balance between technology and ethics. Especially in the development and design of AI, I aim to figure out how to prioritise accessibility and responsibility towards users, ensuring that technology is not only innovative but also in line with social values.
