How a machine can do literature

18 nov 20242024 年 11 月 18 日 · 3 min read约 3 分钟读完

这篇文章写于在学校的课程期间，原文是英文。

My Opinion

Nowadays in this technological era, mathematics is not only a tool for calculation; it has also become an assistant in the field of literary creation.

（Image generated by DouAI）

Through complex algorithms, mathematical machines can automatically generate texts and even write many highly creative contents. Technologies such as machine learning and natural language processing, combined with mathematical formulas and patterns, enable machines to simulate the human writing process.

The principle of mathematical machines lies in using algorithmic rules to combine and arrange words and sentences, thereby generating texts that are logically coherent and grammatically correct. They can analyse a large amount of text data, extract its structure and style, and generate works that conform to a specific style. Although these machines cannot truly understand emotions or meanings, they can create content that stimulates thought or provides inspiration for writers. For example, I tried to let AI generate a passage of poetry:

The quiet moon whispers gently,

Across the silver sea it glides slowly.

A restless breeze dances lightly,

While the shadows of the night fade quietly.

In this example, the machine generates poetry by randomly combining adjectives, nouns, verbs, and adverbs through algorithms. This program selects words from different vocabularies and arranges them into sentences according to set grammatical rules. Although it has no emotional understanding, it imitates the form of poetry creation through mathematics and language rules. I can also set specific themes or emotions for the program, allowing the machine to generate works of a specific style. However, the poetry created still lacks the depth and emotional resonance that comes from human experience, and it is fundamentally driven by patterns rather than genuine feeling (McCormack et al., 2019).


I think that in the future, machines may not only be tools to assist in writing but also become important collaborators in literary creation. Through this human-machine combination, we can look forward to a new literary era full of infinite possibilities and novelty. As AI continues to evolve, its role in the creative process could shift from that of a mere tool to an active participant in the development of new forms of literature. For example, AI can generate unique storylines, suggest character developments, or even help writers explore alternative narrative structures, all while learning from human input (Elgammal et al., 2017). This collaboration could lead to groundbreaking works that blend human creativity with the computational power of machines, potentially reshaping the literary landscape in ways we have yet to fully imagine.
