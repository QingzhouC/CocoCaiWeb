Interactive Installation for Experiencing the Emotions of Abandoned Dogs

13 jan 20252025 年 1 月 13 日 · 4 min read约 4 分钟读完

这篇文章写于在学校的课程期间，原文是英文。

Overview

The aim of the project I’m working on is to create an interactive installation that allows participants to experience the emotions of abandoned dogs through touch and sound. The purpose of this immersive experience design is to evoke empathy, enabling people to feel from the perspective of dogs. Participants need to touch a specially designed sensory floor with their hands and knees, and different areas will trigger sound effects and tactile feedback, simulating the emotional journey of dogs.

Interactive Design

The core of the interaction lies in physical participation. The installation will include a matrix of tactile areas. Participants can activate sound and tactile feedback by placing their hands and knees on designated areas, such as the sound of a door closing or a car starting. This design simulates the process of chasing a moving vehicle, allowing users to experience the anxiety, despair, and helplessness of dogs. Each emotion is associated with specific sensory experiences, from rough textures to cold metal surfaces, creating a dynamic sensory narrative.

Details of Interactive Design

Limb Touch Areas

The floor will be divided into many small square areas from a long rectangle, and each area corresponds to different parts of the story. Each area will activate different feedback according to the limbs (hands and knees) placed by the participants. For example, when the hands touch the “door” area, the sound of the door closing will be played. Similarly, when the knees touch the “road surface” area, a rough surface (such as sandpaper or textured rubber) will simulate the feeling of running on the road. This interaction helps build the tension of chasing the car.

Tactile Feedback

Each area will have a unique tactile experience to match the context of the emotion. For example, the “owner leaving” area can use a cold metal surface to simulate the feeling of being abandoned by the owner. On the contrary, the “road surface” area can use a rough surface to reflect the feeling of running eagerly. These materials will enhance the immersion of emotions and enable participants to feel the emotions of dogs more deeply.

Sound Feedback:

Sound design plays a crucial role in setting the emotional atmosphere. When participants touch different areas, they will hear the sounds of the car starting, accelerating, and gradually moving away. The gradual disappearance of the sound reflects the emotional loss and helplessness of the dog when it is abandoned.

Layered Experience

The installation will be designed as a step-by-step activation process. As participants move on the floor, different sound scenes and tactile feedback will be activated in turn, simulating the process of chasing the car. Each layer of interaction intensifies the emotions of anxiety and helplessness, from the sound of the door closing, the sound of the car starting, to the car gradually accelerating and disappearing. Each layer makes participants feel the disappointment, anxiety, and finally the helplessness of the dog.

User Experience and Immersion

The goal of this interactive installation is to create an emotional connection through body touch and sound. By using touch and sound as the main interaction methods, the installation reduces the need for additional devices (such as VR or AR helmets), making the experience more natural and accessible. Touching the rough road surface or cold metal surface will make the emotional impact more real, helping participants experience the emotional journey of dogs in a more vivid way.

The purpose of the interactive design is to make the experience intuitive and impactful. Without complicated operation instructions, participants will naturally understand that they can activate the progress of the story by touching various areas. The feedback provided is simple yet powerful, allowing users to easily understand and empathize with the emotional journey of dogs.

Summary

By combining technology with narrative design, I can create an immersive experience that allows participants to step into the perspective of abandoned dogs. Through carefully designed interactions, tactile feedback, and sound effects, the installation not only immerses participants in the story but also encourages them to reflect on the themes of abandonment, empathy, and helplessness. This project has deepened my understanding of how interactive installations can create intense and emotionally resonant experiences.
