Final Reflection: Winchester Cathedral AR Project

21 may 20252025 年 5 月 21 日

这篇文章写于在学校的课程期间，原文是英文。

1. Overall Learning Achievements and Challenges

Participating in the Winchester Cathedral AR project was the most complex yet rewarding experience during my creative computing studies. Throughout the semester, I practically integrated AR development, spatial narrative, and interactive design, combining technical implementation with historical and cultural storytelling. The project required me to integrate 3D modelling, Unity AR tools, sound design, and user experience thinking to build an immersive experience with historical authenticity.

One of my greatest achievements was transforming abstract historical themes into interactive visual experiences. From initially researching the architectural evolution of the cathedral to designing functional modules like stained glass window fragmentation and a digital portrait of Jane Austen, I gradually mastered how to create AR scenes that were both technically feasible and emotionally expressive. I also enhanced my visual expression skills through Procreate animated arrows and click prompts I drew not only strengthened user interactivity but also formed an artistic style that harmonized with the cathedral’s atmosphere.

Of course, the process was filled with challenges. The most difficult part was balancing visual goals with development feasibility within technical constraints. My initial idea was to create a complete VR experience that would take users on a time-traveling tour of the cathedral, but I quickly realized that modeling the entire building across different periods was too massive and beyond the project’s scope. The pivotal shift from VR to AR allowed me to leverage the real-world environment, reducing modeling pressure while enhancing interactive flexibility. Another technical hurdle was mobile AR compatibility especially camera permission issues during iOS testing, which forced me to continuously adjust and test alternative demonstration methods.

2. Reflections on Individual Contributions

In this project, I served as both the lead designer and developer, independently completing almost all core modules from early conceptual sketches and interactive flow design to functional implementation and animation import in Unity. One of my most significant contributions was constructing four key functional prototypes: the stained glass window fragmentation experience, the Jane Austen digital portrait, the basement sound sculpture interaction, and the AR arrow navigation system.

Each module was tied to specific historical or emotional objectives. For example, the shattered stained glass symbolizes the fragmentation and loss of memory, while Jane Austen’s digital portrait evokes the character’s presence through sound. I also personally drew interactive prompt animations in Procreate such as red dot click markers, blue arrows, and green endpoint arrows to make the entire experience more delicate and warm. Additionally, I independently researched Vuforia Area Targets technology, providing a feasible roadmap for the project’s subsequent spatial positioning deployment.

Although this was an independently developed project, I frequently sought feedback from peers to ensure each function was both technically viable and aligned with narrative logic. I believe finding balance between independent development and feedback iteration has been a crucial step in my growth as a creative technology practitioner.

3. Technical Platforms and Creative Possibilities: Key Insights

This project has taught me that technical platforms like Unity, AR Foundation, Vuforia, and Procreate are not constraints on creation, but rather co-creators that spark creativity. Each platform has shaped the final effects and ideas I could achieve. For example, Unity’s real-time engine allowed me to dynamically overlay different historical elements, while Vuforia’s Area Targets technology showed me how to make virtual content truly fit into real architectural spaces.

What surprised me most was Procreate. Although it isn’t a traditional AR tool, it became crucial for me to conceive and validate visual rhythms, interactive prompts, and animation styles. Many hand-drawn sketches later translated into interactive animations in Unity, demonstrating the natural synergy between hand-drawn art and technology.

Additionally, I realized that technical limitations can actually help focus on key expressions. Since I couldn’t model the entire cathedral, I began to wonder, which historical fragments must be visible? When is the most appropriate time to present them? Eventually, I adopted a triggered path navigation system, allowing users to gradually understand the history under controlled rhythms. This made me recognize that technical constraints can sometimes drive narrative clarity and focus.

4. Future Development Plan for the Final Major Project

For my graduation project, I plan to further deepen this project, especially in integrating technical deployment with spatial narrative. First, I will complete a spatial anchoring system based on LiDAR + Vuforia Area Targets. By scanning the cathedral’s interior and importing it into Unity, I aim to achieve persistent positioning and occlusion simulation for arrow navigation and sculpture interactions, enhancing the immersive experience.

Second, I hope to expand the character narrative layer. Jane Austen is just the starting point going forward, I intend to add virtual roles like medieval monks, Gothic masons, or pilgrims. These characters can serve as narrators, guiding users through history from diverse perspectives.

I also aim to implement an interactive timeline feature, allowing users to freely switch between different historical periods (such as 1066, the 14th century, and modern restoration). This will enable comparisons of architectural styles, structures, and social contexts, enhancing both educational value and the perceptible changes in spatial dynamics.

Finally, I plan to incorporate remote WebAR access, enabling those unable to visit the cathedral in person to engage in this immersive historical journey from home. This will expand the project’s cultural sharing scope, supporting education, dissemination, and popularization.

In summary, this project has not only enhanced my technical capabilities in AR development and spatial narrative but also taught me how to balance creative visions with implementation paths. It has clarified my future direction: to become a creator who uses digital media to tell spatial stories and evoke historical resonance.
