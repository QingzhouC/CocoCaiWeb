Between Reality and the Impossible

14 jan 20252025 年 1 月 14 日 · 2 min read约 2 分钟读完

这篇文章写于在学校的课程期间，原文是英文。

Overview

“Between Reality and the Impossible” delves deep into how design, technology, and society converge to shape our future. As a student majoring in creative computing, this article has made me reexamine the role of design. It is not merely a tool for achieving functionality but also a medium that sparks imagination and explores future possibilities.

The article challenges the traditional view of design as merely a means to make technology more appealing and consumable. Instead, it advocates using design to pose questions, inspire, and envision alternative futures. This perspective coincides with what I’ve learned in my studies, where I’ve come to understand that design can be a powerful tool for exploring “what-if” scenarios. For instance, design isn’t just about the “future that might happen,” but can also explore “more desirable, plausible, and possible futures.” This mindset encourages me to go beyond immediate practicality and create projects that not only solve existing problems but also reshape the way we live and interact in the long run.

The article emphasizes that technological advancements must be examined from perspectives beyond their functional potential, including their ethical, cultural, and social consequences. This aligns with my growing understanding that design decisions can either perpetuate or challenge existing norms. An impressive example mentioned in the article is speculative projects addressing overpopulation and food shortages. These projects explore extreme possibilities such as synthetic biology and DIY solutions, while also facing the technological and social dimensions of global challenges. This approach inspires me to think about projects from a holistic perspective, considering not only their immediate impacts but also their broader social implications.

The article also highlights the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration in future design. The author encourages designers to collaborate with experts in fields such as ethics, philosophy, and biology to ensure a more comprehensive and in – depth understanding of challenges and opportunities. This reminds me of the collaborative nature in creative computing. When working with peers from different disciplines, I’ve deeply experienced the significance of diverse perspectives in innovative and far – reaching projects. The article further strengthens my belief that meaningful design stems from dialogue and collective wisdom.

After reading “Between Reality and the Impossible”, I’ve come to understand that design should be regarded as a tool for imagining alternative futures and challenging existing paradigms. Meanwhile, we should constantly pay attention to the broad social and cultural impacts of technological advancements. Finally, by collaborating with experts from different fields, we can enrich the depth and relevance of design projects.
