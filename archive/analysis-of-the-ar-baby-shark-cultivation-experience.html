Analysis of the AR Baby Shark Cultivation Experience

5 mar 20252025 年 3 月 5 日 · 3 min read约 3 分钟读完

这篇文章写于在学校的课程期间，原文是英文。

This week, I tried a seven-day AR special effect cultivation project themed around “Baby Shark,” which uses time-progressive interaction and growth-oriented narrative to create a digital pet system in augmented reality that fosters a sense of companionship and emotional connection.

Core Mechanism: Seven-Day Cultivation Narrative Structure

The experience starts with hatching an egg, where users unlock new interactive features daily by opening the app, creating a companionship-style rhythm. The specific process is as follows:

  1. Day 1: Hatching

Users hatch a baby shark by clicking or waiting. The system tells them the breed (three possibilities), establishing the first layer of individual difference and fated choice.

  1. Day 2: Jump Button Unlocked

A jump button appears beside the baby shark. Users can click to make it jump, simulating the learning of instinctive movements.

  1. Day 3: Direction Control

The direction button unlocks, allowing users to move the shark left/right just like to learning to walk, implying a sense of growth.

  1. Day 4: Water Ball Attack + Companion Debut

Two buttons unlock: “Attack (Water Ball)” and “Move”.

  1. Day 5: Love Interaction

A second baby shark (pink, female character) appears for the first time, creating a preliminary social context.A “love button” emerges, enabling users to shoot love hearts at the other shark simulating emotional communication through expressing affection.

  1. Day 6: Following Behavior

The pink shark starts actively following the protagonist, symbolizing togetherness and designing emotional warmth.

  1. Day 7: Ending Prompt

The screen reminds users “It’s the last day” and encourages them to “adopt a new egg,” forming a closed loop while leaving room for memories and continuation.

My Observations and Insights

  • Rhythmic Interaction Structure：
    Unlocking only one feature per day creates a ritualistic daily update atmosphere, while motivating users to stay engaged continuously.
  • Progressive Interaction and Emotional Bonding：
    Starting from basic actions (jumping, moving) to emotional behaviors (shooting love hearts, following), it gradually builds emotional investment. Each action symbolizes a step of growth, evoking the feeling of accompanying it to grow up.
  • Cute Personified Design：
    Although sharks are marine creatures, their personified presentation (jumping, walking, showing affection) bridges the gap with users, enriching the companion pet concept in AR.
  • AR Integration Approach：
    The overall visuals overlay on the real environment via the phone camera, creating a sense of presence that it’s right beside me, which is a perfect paradigm for lightweight mobile AR experiences.

Relevance to My Project (Winchester Cathedral AR)

The seven-day cultivation system inspired me to consider: Must narratives be continuous? Can they instead adopt phased growth and modular unfolding? For example, could visitors activate historical fragments in different areas to unlock stories about Jane Austen or saints, similar to how features are unlocked in the shark experience?

Additionally, its design of user behavior guidance is highly effective, new buttons appear with immediately triggerable functions, reminding me to enhance the visibility and intuitiveness of interactive prompts in my own project.
