What should artificial intelligence be used for?

12 jan 20252025 年 1 月 12 日 · 4 min read约 4 分钟读完

这篇文章写于在学校的课程期间，原文是英文。

Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming various industries, reshaping society, and even having an unprecedented impact on our personal lives. However, with this transformation comes a crucial question that we need to consider: What should artificial intelligence be used for?

(Source: The Fusioneer, n.d., The Fusioneer, Available at: https://thefusioneer.com/blog/the-future-of-ai-5-ai-advancements-to-expect-in-the-next-10-years/)

I believe AI can be used to solve complex problems, enhance human creativity, and improve the efficiency of daily life.

One of the most promising applications of artificial intelligence is to solve complex problems that are data-intensive and beyond the scope of human capabilities. In fields such as healthcare, climate change, and the economy, AI is capable of processing vast amounts of data, identifying patterns, and proposing solutions that can have a significant impact on our world. For example, in the field of climate science, AI models can predict environmental changes more accurately, providing insights into weather patterns, sea-level rise, and other key climate factors. This information is crucial for formulating policies to mitigate the effects of climate change and adapt to its consequences (Rolnick et al., 2019).

AI is also being used to enhance human creativity. Artists, musicians, writers, and designers are leveraging AI tools to explore new forms of art, music, and literature that might not be achievable without the help of these intelligent systems. For example, generative AI can create entirely new visual artworks, compose music, or generate text, all by learning from existing creative works. AI doesn’t replace human creativity; instead, it serves as a powerful tool to inspire and enhance the creative process. By collaborating with AI, artists can push the boundaries of what’s possible and experiment with new ideas and techniques. This collaboration between human imagination and machine intelligence can lead to innovation and expand the limits of our creativity (Elgammal et al., 2017).

AI’s ability to automate daily tasks and optimise processes has made our lives more efficient. From personal assistants like Siri and Alexa to AI recommendation systems on streaming platforms, AI has been integrated into our daily routines, making interactions smoother and more personalised. For example, in the workplace, AI can take over mundane administrative tasks such as scheduling, data entry, and customer service, allowing human employees to focus on more complex, creative, and strategic work. This not only boosts productivity but also enhances employee job satisfaction, as they can spend more time on meaningful tasks (Brynjolfsson and McAfee, 2014).


Beyond that, AI plays a transformative role in improving accessibility and inclusivity. For people with disabilities, AI-driven tools such as speech recognition, text-to-speech, and computer vision systems can break down barriers and create a more inclusive environment. For instance, AI systems capable of recognizing and interpreting sign language can facilitate communication for the deaf or hard-of-hearing (Hochberg et al., 2018). Moreover, AI can assist in creating smarter cities that are more responsive to the needs of all citizens. From enhancing public transportation systems to making urban spaces more accessible, AI is able to foster a more equitable society (Albino et al., 2015).

Of course, finally, as the capabilities of AI grow, we also need to take on more responsibility to ensure its ethical use. The use of AI should benefit society while minimising potential harm. This includes avoiding biases in AI algorithms, ensuring privacy and security, and addressing the issue of job losses caused by automation. At the same time, it is crucial to ensure that AI remains transparent and accountable in the decision-making process. AI should never be used to manipulate, deceive, or violate individual rights. Its deployment should follow principles that prioritise human well-being and are consistent with social values. The responsible use of AI can unlock its huge potential while avoiding adverse consequences. By using AI responsibly, we can create a future where technology serves to enhance, rather than undermine, the human experience.
