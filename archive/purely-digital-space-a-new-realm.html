Purely Digital Space – A New Realm

18 nov 20242024 年 11 月 18 日 · 3 min read约 3 分钟读完

这篇文章写于在学校的课程期间，原文是英文。

Overview

In nowadays digital age, the pure digital space has become an indispensable part of our lives.

(Source: Henderson, 2022, Fierce Pharma, Available at: https://www.fiercepharma.com/marketing/swipe-up-for-pharma-consumers-see-more-facebook-and-instagram-ads-but-search-still-rules).）


Social media platforms are typical examples of how technology has transformed communication. On these platforms, we can instantly share our experiences and establish connections with people across the globe. Time seems to have lost its traditional boundaries, as we can access past memories and engage in real-time conversations simultaneously. This blur between past and present is facilitated by the constant flow of information, where interactions are no longer confined to a specific moment but are instead woven into an ongoing digital narrative (Smith, 2018). As a result, social media has reshaped how we perceive and experience time, offering new opportunities for connectivity and communication (Jones, 2020).

Virtual reality technology has enabled us to venture into even further fields. From early attempts like Sensorama to the advanced head-mounted display devices available today, it provides us with immersive experiences and transports us into digital worlds free from physical constraints. Through VR, we can explore new virtual worlds and interact with digital environments in ways that were once unimaginable (Milgram and Kishino, 1994). This technology opens new realms for exploration and personal engagement, revolutionizing fields such as entertainment, education, and simulation (Slater and Wilbur, 1997).

The Internet is at the heart of this digital revolution, allowing us to access vast amounts of information, communicate freely with others, and collaborate on a global scale. Technologies like TikTok’s algorithm have made digital experiences highly personalised, tailoring content to individual preferences, making it feel as if each of us has our own exclusive broadcasting channel (Zeng et al., 2020). This level of personalisation further deepens the immersive quality of our digital experiences and enhances our connection with online platforms.

The pure digital space is not just a collection of bits and bytes. It is a vibrant and constantly evolving field that offers infinite possibilities for creativity, learning, and connection. It challenges our traditional concepts of time and space and allows us to explore new things. As technology continues to advance, the potential of the pure digital space will keep expanding and shape the future in ways that we can't yet fully imagine.
